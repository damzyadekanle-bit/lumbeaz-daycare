Privacy Policy

Information about how tour request details are handled.

Effective Date: August 3, 2026

At Lumbeaz Daycare, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the steps we take to safeguard it.

Information We Collect

When you contact us or submit a Schedule a Tour request, we may collect information such as:

  • Parent or guardian name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Child's age
  • Program of interest
  • Preferred tour date and time
  • Any additional information you choose to provide

We may also collect limited technical information, such as your browser type and device information, to help improve our website.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your inquiries
  • Schedule and confirm daycare tours
  • Provide information about our programs and services
  • Improve our website and user experience
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

We will only use your information for these purposes.

Information Sharing

Your privacy is important to us.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We may share your information only with trusted service providers that help us operate our website or process tour requests, such as our secure online form provider, when necessary.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the personal information you provide against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or misuse.

Although no method of transmitting information over the internet is completely secure, we are committed to using appropriate safeguards to protect your data.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve website functionality and better understand how visitors use our site.

You can disable cookies through your browser settings if you prefer.

Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for parents and legal guardians.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through this website.

Third-Party Services

Our website may include services provided by trusted third parties, including:

  • Google Maps – to help families locate our daycare.
  • Formspree – to securely process contact and tour request forms.

These third-party services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

Your Privacy Choices

You may request to:

  • Access the personal information you have submitted.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Request that we delete your information, where permitted by law.

To make a request, please contact us using the information below.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
